- 🪐 **Gravitational Orbital Slingshot**: Tap and hold to tether your ship to nearby celestial bodies and swing around them with centrifugal acceleration.
- 🎵 **Harmonic Pentatonic Synth Chords**: Every latch and release generates melodious musical synth tones that harmonize with the background soundtrack.
- 🌌 **Cosmic Asteroid Hazards**: Dodge floating space debris and navigate narrow orbital gates.

| **Release / Slingshot** | Release Click or `[SPACE]` | Release Touch |
| **Restart Match** | Click `RETRY` / `[SPACE]` | Tap `RETRY` button |
